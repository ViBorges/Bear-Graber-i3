Prusa MK2/MK2S/MK2.5/MK3 Arduino MEGA 2560 RAMPS Enclosure for VSLOT 2020 by fmendoza06 on Thingiverse: https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:3345091

Summary:
This is a modification to the RAMBO card box that comes with the Prusa i3 M3 Bear for Aluminum Profile VSLOT 2040, also for 2020.The improvement is made for the Arduino Mega 2560 with RAMPS 1.4The window for the cables of the 12864 LCD display for RAMPS 1.4 was uncovered. The box was tested and perfectly printed without any inconvenience, the supports of the original design of Prusa were adapted.
