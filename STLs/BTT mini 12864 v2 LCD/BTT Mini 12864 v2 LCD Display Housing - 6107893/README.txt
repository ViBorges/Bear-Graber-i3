BTT Mini 12864 v2 LCD Display Housing by evghenix on Thingiverse: https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:6107893

Summary:
Finding suitable housing for my Mini 12864 v2 LCD display from BIGTREETECH was challenging. The original version didn't align perfectly, so I had to make adjustments. I fixed the mounting holes and included a button knob to address the spacing issue caused by the longer reset button on the BTT display. You need to print 2 mount brackets one normally and one mirrored.
